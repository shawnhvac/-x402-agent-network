## Key Decisions Made

1. **API-First Architecture** - ChatGPT plugin is primary distribution, not app
2. **OpenAPI Fallback** - Payment resilience with dual processors
3. **Daily Payouts** - Automated at 6 AM UTC for provider happiness
4. **Tiered Fees** - 1-3% based on transaction size (competitive)
5. **Free Trial Model** - 30 days before asking for payment
6. **Email Notifications** - 4 types (payment, booking, payout, failure)
7. **Cron Scheduler** - Daily batch processing instead of real-time
