### Architecture Review

**Flow**:
1. Vehicle detects health issue (engine degradation over 30+ days)
2. Autonomously requests quotes from all available mechanics
3. NegotiationEngine scores each offer:
   - Bob (85 rep, 100 USDC, 120 min) = ~78/100
   - Alice (90 rep, 120 USDC, 90 min) = ~82/100 ✅ SELECTED
   - Charlie (70 rep, 80 USDC, 150 min) = ~71/100
4. Auto-selects best offer (Alice, 82/100 score)
5. Creates escrow automatically
6. Mechanic accepts job, performs work
7. Completes milestone, triggers payment release
8. Vehicle confirms delivery, updates own condition
9. Mechanic receives payment + reputation increase

**Key Innovation**: Entire transaction requires zero human decision-making. Agents discover, negotiate, agree, execute, and settle autonomously.
