## Quick Logging Guide

**Every evening at 8 PM UTC:**
1. Check Coinbase for grid fills in last 24h
2. Calculate gross profit (total filled orders)
3. Subtract Coinbase fees (usually 0.1-0.5% per fill)
4. Log market condition briefly
5. Add any notes (e.g., "ETH grid triggered 3x", "Volatile day")

**Takes 2 minutes. No exceptions.**
