## 🏗️ WHAT WE'RE BUILDING

### Three Components

**1. The Marketplace**
- Service discovery (ChatGPT finds the best salon in your area)
- Provider profiles (ratings, availability, pricing)
- Smart ranking (agents prefer our ranking over Google)
- Mobile app for providers to manage bookings

**2. The API**
- ChatGPT plugins (ChatGPT can call our booking endpoint)
- Google Assistant integration (Google can call our endpoint)
- Siri Shortcuts (Siri can call our endpoint)
- OpenAPI payment standard (agents get HTTP 402 for payment)

**3. The Payment Infrastructure**
- Stripe integration (consumer payments)
- Solana smart contracts (agent escrow)
- Provider payouts (daily or weekly)
- Smart fee calculation (2-3% per booking)
