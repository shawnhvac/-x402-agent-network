## The Complete Story (In Order)

### How We Got Here
1. Started with vague "AI marketplace" idea
2. Identified that users bring their own AI (ChatGPT, Siri, Google)
3. Realized we should be API, not app
4. Found that credit cards are needed, not crypto
5. Discovered we can own data (OpenStreetMap) for free
6. **Breakthrough:** Providers won't pay without proof
7. **Solution:** Free trials fix the cold-start problem
8. **Result:** Defensible, scalable, fundable business

## Critical Insights (Don't Forget)

1. **Don't build the AI** → Users bring their own
2. **Don't ask for money upfront** → Prove value first (free trials)
3. **Own your data** → Use OpenStreetMap, not Google Maps
4. **Create dependency** → That's the real moat
5. **Test before scaling** → Validation-first approach
6. **Network effects matter** → More providers = exponential growth
