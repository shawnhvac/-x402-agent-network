## 🎯 **What We're Building**

### **AgentPay™ Platform**

A two-sided marketplace where:
- **Service Providers (Agents)** earn money offering HVAC, plumbing, mechanical, electrical, and custom services
- **Consumers (Individuals + Businesses)** book services instantly via voice or text
- **Payments** are locked in SmartEscrow until service completion
- **Settlement** happens on Solana mainnet in USDC (stablecoin, no volatility)

**Why it matters:**
- ✅ Eliminates payment risk (escrow)
- ✅ Instant settlement (Solana, not 2-week delays)
- ✅ Zero platform fees (competitive advantage)
- ✅ Transparent (all transactions on-chain)
- ✅ Scalable (handles millions of transactions)
