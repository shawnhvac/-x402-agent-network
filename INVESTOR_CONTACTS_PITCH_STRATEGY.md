## 🎯 Pitch Sequencing Strategy

### **Week 1-2: Warm Intros Only**
- Reach out to contacts who can introduce you
- Friends, advisors, other founders
- Target: 5-10 warm intros
- Goal: Get 2-3 coffee meetings

### **Week 3-4: Follow-up + Cold Outreach**
- Follow up on warm intros
- Send cold emails to non-responsive tier 2 VCs
- Target: 15-20 outreach total
- Goal: 5-7 investor meetings scheduled

### **Week 5-8: Active Pitching**
- Pitch 2-3 investors per week
- Refine pitch based on feedback
- Share new metrics/progress
- Target: Aim for 2-3 term sheet interest

### **Month 3+: Negotiation**
- Narrow to 1-2 lead investors
- Negotiate terms
- Close funding
