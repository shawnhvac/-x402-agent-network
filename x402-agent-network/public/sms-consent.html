SMS Consent & Opt-In

Last Updated: April 22, 2026

This page describes how AgentPay collects consent to send SMS (text message) notifications to service providers and customers, and explains exactly what messages you will receive.

1. Who Sends the Messages

SMS messages are sent by AgentPay (x402-agent-pay.com) from the phone number +1 (866) 401-6083. AgentPay is an AI-powered service marketplace that connects customers with local service providers.

2. How You Opt In

Service Providers opt in when they register their business on the AgentPay platform at x402-agent-pay.com/register. During registration, providers check a box explicitly agreeing to receive SMS booking notifications from AgentPay. No messages are sent unless the provider has completed registration and provided their mobile number.
Customers opt in via text by texting START to +1 (866) 401-6083, or by checking the consent checkbox during the booking checkout flow on the AgentPay website.

5. How to Opt Out

You can opt out of SMS notifications at any time by replying STOP to any message from AgentPay. You will receive one final confirmation that you have been unsubscribed. No further messages will be sent.

To re-subscribe, reply START to +1 (866) 401-6083.

6. Help

Reply HELP to any AgentPay message for assistance, or contact us at X402agentpay@gmail.com.

7. Message & Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier and plan. AgentPay does not charge for SMS messages; however, your carrier's standard messaging rates may apply.

8. Privacy

Your phone number is used solely to send the booking notifications described on this page. We do not sell or share your phone number with third parties for marketing purposes. See our full Privacy Policy for details.
