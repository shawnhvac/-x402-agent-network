How It Works

Autonomous end-to-end — from discovering a provider to confirmed booking

1

You set your preferences

Tell your agent your preferences once — preferred providers, max budget, time windows, service frequency. It remembers everything.

2

Agent discovers providers

Your agent searches AgentPay's marketplace using the x402 protocol — finding verified, bookable providers near you with real pricing and availability.

3

Agent negotiates and books

The agent compares options, finds the best value, and books automatically — paying the service fee via USDC micropayment on Base mainnet.

4

You get notified — that's it

You receive a confirmation. No phone calls, no back-and-forth. Your agent handled everything while you were doing something else.
