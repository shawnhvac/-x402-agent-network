Get Started in 3 Steps

Sideload the APK — no app store needed right now

1

Enable Unknown Sources

Go to Settings → Security → Install Unknown Apps and allow your browser to install APKs. This is a one-time setting.

2

Download & Install the APK

Tap the Download button above or visit x402-agent-pay.com/downloads/agentpay-provider.apk. Open the downloaded file and tap Install.

3

Register Your Business

Open the app, enter your business name, phone number, and service categories. Claim your OSM listing to get Verified and start receiving bookings.

Requirements
