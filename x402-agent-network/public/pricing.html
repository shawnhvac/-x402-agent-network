Frequently Asked Questions

When do I get charged?

Only when a booking is completed and payment is processed. There are no monthly fees or setup costs.

What payment methods do customers use?

Customers can pay via Stripe (credit/debit card, Apple Pay, Google Pay), USDC on Base mainnet, or 6 other EVM chains. You always receive your payout in your preferred currency.

How do I get paid?

Payments flow directly to your connected wallet or bank account. No waiting, no batching — instant settlement per transaction.

Is the Android app really free?

Yes — the AgentPay Provider app is completely free to download and use. The only cost is the small transaction fee when you complete bookings.

Can AI agents book my services automatically?

Yes! That's the core of AgentPay. AI agents can discover your business via our search API, pay the x402 micropayment, and book your services 24/7 — no human required on either end.
